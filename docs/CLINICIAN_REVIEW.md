# Clinician review of education content

## Why
The 12 education cards in `lib/content/education.dart` were written
with reference to widely available clinical guidelines, but they have
not been reviewed by a clinician. Before publishing, they must be
reviewed by someone with clinical experience in PCOS care.

## Who
Options in order of preference:
1. Your loved one's own gynecologist or endocrinologist (best — they
   know her case)
2. A PCOS-specialist clinician via a paid consultation
3. A registered dietitian or nurse practitioner with PCOS experience
4. A medical reviewer service (e.g. specialist freelance reviewers)

## What to send them
Copy the contents of `lib/content/education.dart` into a document with
this framing:

> Hi — I've built a free wellness tracking app for people with PCOS.
> It's not a medical device and it doesn't diagnose or treat anything.
> One feature is a small library of education cards. I'd love your
> eyes on the copy for factual accuracy and tone. I'm not asking you
> to endorse the app — just to flag anything that's wrong or
> overstates what's known. Happy to pay for your time.

## What you're asking for
- [ ] Factual accuracy of each claim
- [ ] No overstatement of evidence
- [ ] No dosing advice or supplement promotion
- [ ] Warm, non-judgmental tone
- [ ] At least one sentence per card that a clinician would sign

## What you are NOT asking for
- Endorsement or "reviewed by" credit (unless they offer it)
- Legal medical advice about your app
- Approval of the app's features
