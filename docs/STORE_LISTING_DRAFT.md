## Full description

Lunora is a wellness tracker built specifically for people living with
PCOS — not a period tracker with extra screens.

**What it does**
- Log symptoms across hormonal, metabolic, mental, and cycle categories
- Track medications and supplements with a simple daily check
- Record periods as ranges, not false-precision dates
- See your cycle length range, not a single "predicted" date
- Export a doctor-ready PDF summary before appointments
- Read short, clinician-sourced education cards

**What it does NOT do**
- No ovulation prediction (PCOS makes this unreliable and misleading)
- No "your period is late" alerts (variation is expected)
- No weight-loss claims
- No ads
- No paywall. Every feature is free.
- No account required. Data stays on your device.

**Who it's for**
People with a PCOS diagnosis, people exploring whether they might have
PCOS, and anyone whose cycles don't fit the 28-day default.

**Privacy**
Lunora stores everything on your device. Nothing is uploaded. If you
export a PDF, you choose where it goes. Uninstalling the app deletes
everything.

**Disclaimer**
Lunora is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat PCOS.
Always talk to a clinician about medical decisions.
