# Lunora — Medical Disclaimer

**This is the canonical disclaimer that appears throughout the app.**

Lunora is a wellness tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not
diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) or
any other condition.

Lunora does not predict ovulation, fertile windows, or future period
dates. The "likely window" shown on the Cycle screen is a **descriptive
range** computed from cycles you have already logged. It is not a
prediction.

Education content in Lunora is general information, not personalized
medical advice. It was written with reference to widely available
clinical guidelines, but it has not been reviewed by your clinician.

**Always talk to a qualified healthcare professional before making any
changes to your care plan, medications, or treatment.**

If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis
line in your country.

## Where this disclaimer appears
- Onboarding screen 5 (with required checkbox)
- Settings tab (permanent card)
- PDF report footer on every page
- Education detail modal footer

## What it must NOT say
- "Lunora helps you balance hormones." (implies medical effect)
- "Lunora can help you get pregnant." (implies fertility function)
- "Trusted by doctors." (false authority)
- "Clinically proven." (no such evidence exists for this app)
