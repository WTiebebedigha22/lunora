# Lunora Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [FILL IN DATE]**

## What Lunora is
Lunora is a wellness tracking app for people living with PCOS. It is a
tool for personal record-keeping. It is not a medical device and does
not diagnose or treat any condition.

## What data Lunora collects

### Data you enter
When you log symptoms, cycles, medications, or read education cards,
Lunora stores that data **only on your device**. It is not sent to us
or to any third party.

### Data Lunora does NOT collect
- No name, email, or account. There is no sign-up.
- No location data.
- No advertising identifiers.
- No analytics that identify you.
- No crash reports that include your data.
- No usage of your health entries by us, ever.

### Data you choose to share
- The "Export PDF" feature generates a report **on your device**.
  Where you send it (email, print, save) is entirely your choice. We
  do not receive a copy.

## Where your data is stored
All of your entries are stored in an on-device database managed by
Hive, a local storage library. The data lives inside the Lunora app
folder on your device.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of your data. There is no cloud
backup.

## Your rights
- You can export a PDF summary of your data at any time from Settings.
- You can delete all data from Settings → Clear all data. This is
  immediate and irreversible.
- You can uninstall the app, which removes all data.

## Children
Lunora is not intended for anyone under 16.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the app version will bump and the new policy
will be shown on first launch after the update.
